# Vidas

Installers and updates for Vidas. Vidas checks this page by itself and installs new versions, so these downloads are only needed for a first install.

## Vidas 2.3.2

- Mac: [Vidas-2.3.2.dmg](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/drixosio/vidas-releases/main/2.3.2/Vidas-2.3.2.dmg)
- Windows: [Vidas-2.3.2-setup.exe](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/drixosio/vidas-releases/main/2.3.2/Vidas-2.3.2-setup.exe)

- A visual pass over the whole app. Corners follow one scale, and anything rounded inside something rounded now curves in step with it, as in menus, the column view, and the sidebar.
- Every hover, open, and close now moves at the same pace with the same easing.
- The toolbar no longer shifts sideways when you switch to a category short enough not to scroll, and the preview’s buttons stay in place as you move between assets.
- Text fields and dropdowns share one shape, and the app icon is now the small V logo at every size.
- Vidas now keeps sounds: MP3, WAV, AIFF, M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG, and more. They have their own Audio tab, and search finds them with words like sound, music, jingle, or sfx.
- Each sound shows its waveform and length. Click play on a tile or a list row to hear it, or click the waveform to start from that point. Only one sound plays at a time.
- Press Space on a sound to open it large, with a detailed waveform, Loop, and volume. While a sound plays, the arrow keys play the next one, so you can audition a set quickly.
- Columns and the list show a sound’s sample rate, channels, and bit depth. Copy File puts the sound file itself on the clipboard, ready to paste into a folder, Slack, or an editor.
- When assets are sorted by category, click a category’s label to hide it and click again to bring it back. Hide Images and Videos, for example, to see only Logos and Colors. Vidas remembers what you hid.
- Stacks and format sets now work in column view, and a stack opens right there instead of switching to the grid.
- Stacks in the list and in columns have a new look. An arrow opens the stack in place, a closed stack shows its count on the thumbnail, and an open one numbers every version on one band.
